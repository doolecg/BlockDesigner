# Camera, look and feel

## Camera

Orbit and pan like Blender, with a view cube, perspective or orthographic views, numpad views, and creative flight with momentum.

- **Middle-drag** orbits around the point under the mouse.
- **Shift+middle-drag** pans.
- **Wheel** zooms.
- **F** frames the selected blocks, or else the active layer. **Home** frames everything.
- **P** / **O** switch to perspective / orthographic.
- **Numpad 1 / 3 / 7** give front / right / top. Add Ctrl for back / left / bottom.

Flying is covered on [Building like in Minecraft](Building-Like-In-Minecraft). The full camera list is on [Controls and keybinds](Controls-And-Keybinds).

## Themes

Pick a theme in Settings: Claude, Blue, Green, Red, Orange or Zen. Each can be dark, light, or follow Windows. A theme colours every window and the 3D view's sky and grid.

## Viewport settings

Press **N** for the viewport settings. Place, break and paint sounds, break particles and quiet UI sounds are there, and each can be turned off. **Alt+G** turns the ground grid on and off.

## Key hints

Key hints in the corner show the keys for what you're doing. **Shift+F1** turns them on and off. **F1** (or Alt+K) lists all your shortcuts, and **F11** goes full screen.

## Keybinds

Every keyboard shortcut can be changed in **Settings > Keybinds** (**Ctrl+,**). Each action can have a second key, and clashes are flagged.
